How I work

Four steps, repeated every month.

Local rankings move with steady work. Each month we check the numbers and adjust.

  1. Audit

    Check your profile, website, citations and competitors. Run a geo-grid scan to see where you rank street by street.

  2. Fix the foundation

    Correct categories, name, address and phone everywhere. Fix site issues that block indexing.

  3. Build content and trust

    Service and location pages, GBP posts, review requests, and answers to the questions customers ask AI tools.

  4. Track and adjust

    Re-scan rankings, read Search Console and GBP insights, and move effort to what works.
