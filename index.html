AI Search · AEO / GEO

Apex Imaging Centre: becoming the answer in AI search

Problem
Patients now ask AI tools who to visit. Dr. Parth Patel's radiology centre needed to be the name that comes back.
Approach
Wrote answer-first content and FAQs around the questions patients really ask, kept name, address, phone and services identical across Google, the website and directories, and added entity and schema markup so AI tools can understand and cite the centre.
Result
Named as the answer to "Who is the best radiologist in Sargasan?" in AI search and Google.
